# Draft Policy Summary for Counsel

The interactive prototype includes short-form draft policy screens. They are placeholders for legal drafting.

## Terms

- eligibility and accurate account information,
- prohibited cheating/collusion/multi-accounting,
- published competition rules control outcomes,
- final governing law/payment terms TBD.

## Competition rules

- entry/fee/prizes disclosed before entry,
- same core rules/game build for all players,
- server result is official live record,
- voluntary exit after start generally treated as forfeit.

## Disconnect/refund

- cancel before start: no charge,
- voluntary disconnect after start: normally no refund,
- verified server failure with no reliable result: proposed refund,
- integrity concern: hold settlement pending review.

## Fair play

- authoritative servers,
- anti-cheat/risk controls,
- result/replay logging,
- ability to hold flagged settlements.

## Responsible play

- 18+ proposed live competitive access,
- spending limits,
- session reminders,
- competitive pause/self-exclusion pathway,
- no credit/lending mechanics.

## Privacy

Potentially processed categories:

- account/profile,
- KYC/identity status,
- eligibility/location result,
- payment/wallet records,
- device/security signals,
- gameplay/match history,
- support/dispute records.

Retention, lawful basis, user rights, cross-border processing and processor terms require jurisdiction-specific advice.
